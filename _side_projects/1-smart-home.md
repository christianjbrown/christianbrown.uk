* Room-by-room temperature and humidity from SmartThings sensors, set against the UK Met Office forecast, with charted history.
* Serverless functions on Cloud Run behind a Fastly CDN, with readings stored in Cloud SQL and all infrastructure in Terraform.
* Built and open-sourced SDKs for the [SmartThings API](https://github.com/christianjbrown/smartthings-api-sdk-php) and the [Met Office Weather DataHub API](https://github.com/christianjbrown/met-office-weather-datahub-api-sdk-php).
